# Tally Pricing and Plans 2026

Effective 1 January 2026. All prices are in EUR, excluding VAT, per warehouse site per month, billed annually unless stated otherwise.

| Feature | Starter | Growth | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price per site per month | €149 | €449 | Custom, from €1,200 |
| Tags included | 50 | 300 | Unlimited |
| Additional tags | €2.40 per tag per month | €1.90 per tag per month | Included |
| Hubs included | 1 | 3 | Unlimited |
| User seats | 5 | 25 | Unlimited |

| API access | Read-only, 120 requests/min | Full, 600 requests/min | Full, 3,000 requests/min |

| SSO (SAML) | Not available | Not available | Included |

## 2. Billing and discounts

- Plans are billed annually in advance by default. Monthly billing is available at a 15% surcharge.
- Nonprofit and education customers receive 25% off the plan price.
- Volume discounts: 10% off for 10 or more sites, 18% off for 50 or more sites.
- Volume discounts and the nonprofit/education discount do not stack; the larger discount applies.
- Contracts renew automatically. Cancellation notice must be given at least 60 days before the renewal date.

## 4. SLA credits

If monthly uptime for a site falls below the plan's SLA, the customer receives a service credit on the next invoice:

| Monthly uptime | Credit |
|---|---|
| Below the plan SLA but at least 99.0% | 10% of the monthly plan fee |
| Below 99.0% but at least 95.0% | 25% of the monthly plan fee |
| Below 95.0% | 50% of the monthly plan fee |

Credits must be claimed within 30 days of the end of the affected month. Starter plan customers are covered by the SLA but credits are capped at 10%.

## 5. Free trial

New customers can try the Growth plan free for 30 days. During the trial, Brindlewave loans up to 20 TT-200 tags and one hub; loaned hardware must be returned within 14 days of the trial ending or it will be invoiced at list price.
