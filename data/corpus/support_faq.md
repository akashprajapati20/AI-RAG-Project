# Tally Customer Support FAQ

_Maintained by Support Operations (Sofia Brandt). Last updated: 1 September 2026._

## Billing

**Can I get a refund?**
Annual plans can be cancelled for a pro-rated refund within the first 30 days. After 30 days there are no refunds, but you can downgrade at your next renewal. To cancel or downgrade, give notice at least 60 days before the renewal date.

**Can I add more users than my plan includes?**
No. Starter includes 5 user seats and Growth includes 25. To add more users, upgrade your plan. Enterprise has unlimited seats.

## Hardware

**What is the hardware warranty?**
Tags and hubs have a 3-year warranty from the date of shipment. Batteries are not covered. To make a claim, open an RMA in the support portal. Enterprise customers get advance replacement: we ship the replacement within 1 business day. On other plans, we ship the replacement after we receive the faulty unit.

**A tag's LED blinks amber every 30 seconds. What does that mean?**
The battery is below 15%. Replace both CR123A lithium batteries within 2 weeks. The tag reconnects automatically after the battery change and does not need recalibration.

**I moved a tag to another shelf. Do I need to do anything?**
Yes — recalibrate it (Tare with an empty shelf, then Calibrate with the 5 kg reference weight). Readings from a moved tag are wrong until it is recalibrated.

**How many tags can one hub handle?**
Up to 250, but we recommend at most 200 per hub for headroom.

**What happens if the internet connection at my site goes down?**
The hub switches to its LTE fallback if a SIM is installed. If there is no uplink at all, the hub buffers up to 72 hours of readings and uploads them when the connection returns.

## Data and account

**How do I export my data?**
Go to Settings → Data → Export CSV. Exports longer than 90 days currently time out (known issue in 4.3.0); use the API with daily resolution instead.

**Can I move my organisation to another data region?**
Only on the Enterprise plan. Contact your customer success manager; a region migration takes up to 10 business days.

**How long do you keep my data?**
Telemetry is kept for 13 months on Starter and 25 months on Growth and Enterprise. See the Security and Compliance Overview for details.

## Contacting support

- Starter: email support@tally.example, response within 2 business days.
- Growth: email or in-app chat, response within 8 business hours.
- Enterprise: 24/7 phone line, 1-hour response for P1 issues.

Support hours for email and chat are 08:00–18:00 CET (EU) and 08:00–18:00 CT (US), Monday to Friday.
