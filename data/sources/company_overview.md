# Brindlewave — Company Overview

_Last updated: 12 January 2026. Maintained by the Communications team._

Brindlewave B.V. builds **Tally**, a warehouse inventory platform that combines battery-powered shelf sensors with a cloud dashboard and a REST API. Instead of periodic manual stock counts, Tally measures the weight on each shelf section continuously and turns it into live stock levels, low-stock alerts and restock forecasts.

## At a glance

- **Founded:** March 2019 in Rotterdam, the Netherlands
- **Founders:** Ines Vermeulen (CEO) and Tomasz Grabowski (CTO)

- **Offices:** Rotterdam (headquarters), Austin, Texas (Americas, opened 2021) and Singapore (APAC, opened September 2024)
- **Customers:** about 1,900 organisations operating roughly 6,400 warehouse sites

## Products

- **Tally Tag TT-200** — the standard shelf load sensor. A cold-storage variant, the **TT-200C**, is available for freezers and chilled rooms.
- **Tally Hub TH-4** — the gateway that connects tags to the cloud.
- **Tally Cloud** — the web dashboard and REST API.
- **Predictive Restock** — a forecasting add-on, launched in April 2026, that predicts how many days remain until a shelf is empty.

## Customers and markets

Our largest customer segments are third-party logistics providers (38% of revenue), food and beverage distribution (27%), and spare-parts and MRO warehouses (19%). Europe accounts for 58% of revenue, the Americas for 31% and APAC for 11%.

## Mission and values

Our mission is to make every shelf in the world count itself. Our values are:

1. **Measure, don't guess.** We decide with data, and we share it openly.
2. **Own the outcome.** We finish what we start and fix what we break.
3. **Keep it simple.** Hardware and software that a warehouse team can install in an afternoon.
4. **Be kind and direct.** We give honest feedback, respectfully.
